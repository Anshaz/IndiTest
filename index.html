How it works

5-layer scoring (0–5):
1. Price above VWAP — confirms right-side bias
2. RSI crosses above SMA(14) and RSI < 65 — momentum turning, not overbought
3. MACD histogram turning up — downward momentum decelerating
4. Volume above SMA(9) — conviction behind the move
5. Price near Volume Profile HVN — entering at real liquidity support

Alerts fire when: score jumps to 4 or 5, or drops from 5 to 3 or below.
Rate limit: stays under 8 calls/min (free tier). 1 ticker × 5 min = 288 calls/day.
Data: daily OHLCV from Twelve Data. Indicators computed client-side.
